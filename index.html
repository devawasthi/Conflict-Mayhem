Rule Book

  • Each player starts with 1 Blame The Intern and 5 random cards.
  • For n active players, the round targets 2n Production Crash cards and 2n-1 total Blame The Intern cards.
  • Skip is the only card that lets you end the turn without drawing.
  • Nope cancels the latest action or combo unless another Nope stops it.
  • If you draw Production Crash without Blame The Intern, you are out.
  • Sprint Planning makes the next player draw 2 extra cards on their turn.
  • Desk Loot cards have no direct powers on their own, but they drive combo plays.
  • 2 of a kind steals a random card, 3 of a kind asks for a specific card, and 5 different cards reclaim from discard.
  • Watch Room lets you spectate with a room code if you want to watch instead of play.

Gameplay guide

How To Play

1 Setup

Every active player starts with 1 Blame The Intern and 5 random cards.

2 Play

Use one action card or line up a combo before taking the mandatory end-of-turn draw.

3 Pressure

For n active players, the round pushes toward 2n Production Crash cards and 2n-1 total Blame The Intern cards.

2
2 matching cards

Play 2 same cards. Choose a player and steal 1 random card from their hand.

3
3 matching cards

Play 3 same cards. Choose a player, name a card, and take it if they have it.

5
5 different cards

Play 5 different cards. Open the discard pile and reclaim 1 used card back into your hand.

Critical Cards

These decide who survives the release.

Production Crash

Draw this without Blame The Intern and you are eliminated. If the deck ever runs out of crashes while more than one player is alive, the discard pile is folded back in and shuffled so the pressure stays live.

Blame The Intern

Every active player starts with 1. When you draw a Production Crash, this saves you once, then both cards are discarded. The round aims to keep 2n-1 total copies in circulation for n active players.

Nope

Cancels the latest action or combo unless another Nope answers it. It has no standalone turn effect, but it can still be used in combos.

Action Cards

Play these before the mandatory draw.

Peer Review

Look at the next three cards in the deck privately, then decide whether to play more pressure or simply draw.

Skip

The only action that ends your turn without the usual draw. Every other action or combo still leaves the draw step in place.

Sprint Planning

The next player owes 2 extra draws on top of their usual turn draw, while you still draw to end your own turn.

Shuffle

Scramble the live deck. This is most useful after someone studies the top cards or when you want to bury danger deeper.

Project Manager

Choose a player and steal a random card from their hand, then still draw to end your turn.

Desk Loot

These cards do nothing by themselves, but they power the combo system.

Rubber Duck

No direct power on its own. Use Desk Loot cards for combo plays.

Coffee Break

No direct power on its own. Use Desk Loot cards for combo plays.

Sticky Note

No direct power on its own. Use Desk Loot cards for combo plays.

Mechanical Keyboard

No direct power on its own. Use Desk Loot cards for combo plays.

Posh Training

No direct power on its own. Use Desk Loot cards for combo plays.

Round Rules

  • Everyone starts with 1 Blame The Intern and 5 extra cards.
  • For n active players, the round targets 2n Production Crash cards and 2n-1 total Blame The Intern cards.
  • Your turn usually works like this: play 1 action or combo if you want, then draw to end the turn.
  • Skip is the only effect that removes the end-of-turn draw.
  • Sprint Planning makes the next player resolve 3 draws total on a normal turn.
  • 2 matching cards let you choose a player and steal 1 random card from them.
  • 3 matching cards let you choose a player and ask for 1 specific card by name.
  • 5 different cards let you open the discard pile and reclaim 1 used card into your hand.
  • Any non-Production Crash card, including Nope and Blame The Intern, can be used in combos.
  • If the deck has no Production Crash left while multiple players are still alive, the discard pile is shuffled back into the deck immediately.
  • Watch Room lets you spectate with a room code without taking an active player seat.
  • The last player still alive wins the match.
